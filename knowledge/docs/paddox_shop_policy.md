# PADDOX Shop & Return Policy

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-07-30

## Returns and Exchanges
Items purchased from the PADDOX official merchandise store can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and retain all original tags. 

## Non-Returnable Items
Digital goods, including VIP subscription tokens, fantasy team name changes, and exclusive driver avatar packs, are strictly non-refundable.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days globally. Express shipping is available for an additional fee. PADDOX is not responsible for customs delays.
