# PADDOX Platform Guide

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-08-14

## What PADDOX Is
PADDOX is a Formula 1 fan platform that combines race-weekend information, official F1 highlight recommendations, merchandise, team discovery, community features, and digital collectibles in one motorsport-inspired experience.

## Home and Track Mode
The home page's Track Mode presents the next Grand Prix, a circuit map, location, dates, session information, and countdown. The Teams on the Grid section introduces the current constructors. Your Highlights ranks curated videos from the official Formula 1 channel and lets fans dismiss or open recommendations.

## Fan Hub
Fan Pulse includes polls, trivia, community posts, likes, and comments. Fans can earn Fan Points through supported participation such as voting in polls and answering trivia. Community content remains subject to PADDOX moderation rules.

## Shop and Collectibles
Featured Merch links to the PADDOX shop for racewear, posters, accessories, and other products. Race Lab includes the Grand Prix Stamp Wall and PADDOX Badge Cabinet. Digital collectibles can include public verification details and sharing controls; they are not financial investments or blockchain assets.

## AI Pit Wall
The AI Pit Wall retrieves relevant passages from the verified PADDOX and F1 knowledge base before generating an answer. When the knowledge base does not contain enough evidence, it should say so instead of inventing an answer. Source labels shown with an answer come from retrieved documents, not from model-generated citations.
