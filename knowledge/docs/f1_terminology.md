# Curated F1 Terminology

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-07-30

## Common Terms
- **Undercut:** A strategy where a trailing driver pits before the car ahead, using fresh tires to set faster lap times and emerge ahead when the leading car eventually pits.
- **Overcut:** The opposite of an undercut, where a driver stays out on older tires while the car ahead pits, attempting to build a gap before their own stop.
- **Dirty Air:** The turbulent wake of air left behind a leading car, which reduces the aerodynamic downforce of the following car, making it harder to follow closely through corners.
- **Porpoising:** A violent bouncing phenomenon caused by ground-effect aerodynamics, where the car's floor is repeatedly sucked to the track and then stalls, causing it to spring back up.
- **Slipstream:** The area of low air pressure behind a fast-moving car, which a following car can use to reduce drag and gain top speed.
