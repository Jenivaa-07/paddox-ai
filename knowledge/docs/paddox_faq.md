# PADDOX Platform FAQ

Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-07-30

## How are fantasy points calculated?
Points are calculated based on real-world F1 race results. Drivers earn points for their finishing positions (e.g., 25 points for 1st, 18 for 2nd), plus bonus points for the fastest lap, pole position, and overtakes. Negative points are applied for DNFs (Did Not Finish) or penalties incurred during the race.

## How do I join a league?
You can join a public league from the 'Leagues' tab or enter a private league using an invite code provided by the league commissioner.

## Can I change my team mid-season?
Yes, you are granted three "Wildcards" per season, which allow unlimited transfers for that specific race week without penalty. Standard transfers cost 4 points per change after your free transfer allocation is exhausted.
