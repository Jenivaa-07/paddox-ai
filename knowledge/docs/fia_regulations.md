# FIA 2026 F1 Regulations – Section B [Sporting] – Issue 07 – 2026-06-25

Version: Issue 07
Date: 2026-06-25

## 2026 Aerodynamic Rules (Issue 07 Updates)
The FIA mandates that all 2026 cars must feature active aerodynamics, including movable front and rear wings. The drag reduction system (DRS) has been replaced by an overriding low-drag mode deployable on straights, specifically detailed in Issue 07.

## Power Unit Regulations (Issue 07 Updates)
The 2026 power units remove the MGU-H and significantly increase the electrical power output from the MGU-K to 350kW. The internal combustion engine (ICE) remains a 1.6-liter V6 but must run on 100% sustainable fuels. Issue 07 clarifies the energy allocation during qualifying.

## Penalties (Issue 07 Updates)
Any breach of the cost cap or unauthorized wind tunnel testing will result in immediate points deductions in the Constructors' Championship, strictly enforced as of Issue 07.
